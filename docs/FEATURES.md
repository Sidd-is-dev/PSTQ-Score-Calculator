## Calculator

The calculator is the homepage. Stream pages are landings that explain the gate and link into the same calculator.

| Surface | Path | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full calculator | [/](https://pstq.getnorthpath.com/) | Profile form, stream assignment, 1,400-point estimate, hard-gate message, factor breakdown, comparison with the latest published round |
| Stream 1 landing | [/calculator/highly-qualified-skills](https://pstq.getnorthpath.com/calculator/highly-qualified-skills) | TEER 0–2, oral French 7+ / written 5+ |
| Stream 2 landing | [/calculator/intermediate-manual-skills](https://pstq.getnorthpath.com/calculator/intermediate-manual-skills) | TEER 3–5, oral French 5+ |
| Stream 3 landing | [/calculator/regulated-professions](https://pstq.getnorthpath.com/calculator/regulated-professions) | Regulated occupations; French gate follows the TEER band |
| Stream 4 landing | [/calculator/exceptional-talent](https://pstq.getnorthpath.com/calculator/exceptional-talent) | Exceptional talent; French gate marked partial |

On-page anchors on the homepage include the quick FAQ (`/#faq`) and the consultation form (`/#consult`).

## What this product deliberately does not claim

- That it is an official MIFI, Arrima, or IRCC calculator
- That a score above a stated number means you qualify or will be invited
- That English adds PSTQ points
- That a validated job offer is required
- That the PEQ is still open to new applications
- That a CSQ is permanent residence, or that it adds CRS points
- A fixed IRCC processing time in months
- That the general document list is the official MIFI checklist
- That the NOC finder or the CLB table is the official classification or conversion
