# The four PSTQ streams

Every candidate is scored inside **one** stream. The [streams guide](https://pstq.getnorthpath.com/guide/pstq-streams) states that the **1,400-point formula is the same** in all four streams. What changes is the **eligibility gate** and **which invitation pool** you are ranked in.

Stream is decided mainly by **TEER** (NOC 2021) and by whether the occupation is a **regulated profession**. Exceptional talent is a separate, self-declared pathway. You do not choose a stream in order to use a different point table.

Source named on the calculator: Guide des procédures d'immigration, chapter 3, section 1. Confirm current wording on [Québec’s requirements page](https://www.quebec.ca/en/immigration/permanent/skilled-workers/skilled-worker-selection-program/requirements).

Stream landings: [pages/CALCULATOR.md](pages/CALCULATOR.md)

## Stream 1 · Highly qualified and specialized skills

Management and occupations that usually require a university degree or several years of specialized training.

- TEER **0, 1, 2**
- At least **1 year** full-time-equivalent experience in the last **5 years**
- A diploma leading directly to the profession, from a program of at least **one year** full-time
- French: **oral level 7+**, **written level 5+**

Examples the occupation pages place here when the profession is **not** yet on a Stream 3 permit path: [software developers](https://pstq.getnorthpath.com/occupations/software-developers) (typically TEER 1). Nurses and engineers move to Stream 3 when a Québec permit or order requirement applies. See [OCCUPATIONS.md](OCCUPATIONS.md).

---

## Stream 2 · Intermediate and manual skills

The streams guide calls this the **largest stream in every round since July 2026**.

- TEER **3, 4, 5**
- At least **2 years** of paid experience in the last 5 years, **including 1 year in Québec**
- A **secondary diploma or vocational certificate**
- French: **oral level 5+**. No written minimum is stated

The homepage’s initial state (TEER 3, not regulated) is scored in this stream, and the empty-profile gate message uses the oral level 5 minimum.

Occupation pages usually placed here: [orderlies / PAB](https://pstq.getnorthpath.com/occupations/orderlies-pab) (TEER 4), [truck drivers](https://pstq.getnorthpath.com/occupations/truck-drivers) (TEER 3), and many [construction trades](https://pstq.getnorthpath.com/occupations/construction-trades) unless the trade is regulated.

---

## Stream 3 · Regulated professions

For occupations on the Ministry’s list of regulated professions. The site gives examples: nurses, engineers, accountants, and similar.

- **Any TEER**, if the occupation is regulated
- A **valid permit to practice**, or proof of **partial or full equivalence recognition no more than 5 years old**
- French follows the TEER band:
  - TEER 0 to 2: oral **7+** and written **5+**
  - TEER 3 to 5: oral **5+**

The professional-authorization factor in the grid (max **50** points, verified) is separate from this eligibility gate. Holding the permit can be required to be in the stream and can also be worth points. The calculator asks both “is this occupation regulated?” and whether you have a valid permit or equivalence.

---

## Stream 4 · Exceptional talent

For candidates who can demonstrate exceptional expertise likely to contribute to Québec’s prosperity.

- **Any occupation**, self-declared
- An achievement on the ministry’s recognized list, **or** a supporting opinion from a partner body. The site names **MEIE, FRQ, CALQ, or INS**
- At least **3 years** of experience in the last 5 years
- The site calls this the lowest-volume stream and the one with the **least published detail on the French requirement**

The methodology page marks the Stream 4 French gate **partial**: it was not stated in the source excerpt used. The 2026 draw table on the site shows invitation counts for Stream 4 and **no published score** for every round. See [DRAWS.md](DRAWS.md).

## Questions the site answers

**Can I choose my stream?** No. TEER and regulated status decide streams 1 to 3. Exceptional talent has its own eligibility rules.

**Do streams use different point tables?** No. All four use the same 1,400-point grid.

**Does a score in the right stream guarantee an invitation?** No. Each round sets its own range, and that range is not published in advance.
