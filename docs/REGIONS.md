# Regions

Where you live, work, or study changes **regional bonus points** only when it is **outside the Montréal metropolitan area** (the Communauté métropolitaine de Montréal, CMM). The scoring-factors page treats these three rows as **verified**:

| Factor | Maximum |
| --- | --- |
| Residence outside the Montréal metro area | 40 |
| Work experience outside the Montréal metro area | 60 |
| Study duration outside the Montréal metro area | 20 |
| **Combined, at the maximum month bands** | **120** |

The homepage states the same cap in one line: outside the Montréal metro area, up to **120 points** across residence, work, and study. The “+120 pts eligible” labels on Capitale-Nationale, Estrie, and Saguenay–Lac-Saint-Jean mean the region **can** reach that combined maximum. They are not an automatic 120 for selecting the region. Months still have to be entered on the calculator.

Inside the CMM, the site says there is **no regional bonus**. A validated job offer is also location-sensitive: **50 points outside Montréal** and **30 points inside** (the factor itself is verified; the form states both figures).

Index: [pstq.getnorthpath.com/regions](https://pstq.getnorthpath.com/regions). Page README: [pages/REGIONS.md](pages/REGIONS.md).

## How to use this in the calculator

The calculator does not ask you to pick a city from this list. It asks for **months**:

- resident outside the Montréal metro area
- worked outside the Montréal metro area
- studied outside the Montréal metro area

Use a region page to decide whether those months **qualify as outside the CMM**. Then enter the months. The points scale with duration, up to the maxima above.

Each region page tells you to confirm metro status against the current Québec designation before you rely on it, and says the page is not an official Québec result.

## What regional points are not

- Not a separate stream
- Not a substitute for the French hard gate
- Not available for Laval or Longueuil merely because they are outside the Island of Montréal. The site treats both as inside the CMM
- Not a prediction of which region a future Arrima round will target. Rounds can use their own criteria. Compare scores on [DRAWS.md](DRAWS.md), which records score ranges and does not, in the published table, add a region column
