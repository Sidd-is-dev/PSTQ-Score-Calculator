## What each guide is for

**What is the PSTQ?** Defines the Programme de sélection des travailleurs qualifiés, the Arrima expression of interest, and the CSQ. States that the PSTQ replaced the older Programme régulier des travailleurs qualifiés (PRTQ) and absorbed the role the PEQ played before November 2025. A CSQ is not permanent residence. An invitation is not guaranteed by any score.

**The four streams.** How TEER and regulated-profession status assign Stream 1, 2, 3, or 4. Same 1,400-point formula. Detail: [STREAMS.md](../STREAMS.md).

**Scoring factors.** Section maxima and the verified vs partial table. Detail: [SCORING-MODEL.md](../SCORING-MODEL.md).

**PSTQ vs PEQ.** The PEQ is closed to new applications. Québec experience (up to 160) and a Québec diploma (up to 200) still score and no longer create a near-automatic path. The longer tool version is the [PEQ closure check](https://pstq.getnorthpath.com/tools/peq-closure-check).

**Document checklist.** A general, non-exhaustive list: identity and status, French results (TCF Québec, TCF Canada, TEF Canada, TEFAQ, DELF, or DALF, less than 2 years old, or an accepted diploma), education and transcripts with a comparative evaluation if obtained outside Québec, work reference letters with full-time-equivalent hours, a financial self-sufficiency contract covering the first 3 months in Québec, an attestation of learning of democratic and Québec values, and a regulated-profession permit where Stream 3 applies. English results are not scored. Confirm the current list with MIFI. The page says the official checklist varies by stream and can change.
