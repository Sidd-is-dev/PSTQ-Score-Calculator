# Regions hub

**Live:** [https://pstq.getnorthpath.com/regions](https://pstq.getnorthpath.com/regions)

The regions hub compares Québec places for PSTQ **regional bonus** scoring: inside the Montréal metropolitan area versus outside it. It lists **six** profiles and tells you to run the calculator.

Scoring rules and the 40 / 60 / 20 maxima: [REGIONS.md](../REGIONS.md).

---

## What you do here

1. Decide whether the place is inside the Communauté métropolitaine de Montréal
2. If it is outside, the residence, work, and study rows can score, up to 120 points combined at the maximum month bands
3. If it is Montréal, Laval, or Longueuil, the site says those rows do not qualify
4. Enter **months** on the calculator. Choosing a city name on this hub does not itself add points
