# Draws

**Live:** [https://pstq.getnorthpath.com/draws](https://pstq.getnorthpath.com/draws)

The draws page is a structured history of **2026 PSTQ invitation rounds** published on quebec.ca. As checked on 6 October 2026 it covers **nine rounds through 24 September 2026**, all four streams, invitation counts, and published score ranges.

The full table, including the Stream 2 count published as `n/a` on 27 August 2026 and the empty Stream 4 score column, is in [DRAWS.md](../DRAWS.md).

---

## What you do here

1. Find the latest date for **your** stream
2. Read the score range as the lowest and highest **published** threshold across that round’s exercises
3. Use it as context next to your estimate
4. Do not treat the bottom of the range as the score you “need” for a round that has not happened

The page’s own summary: each round invited roughly 500 to 2,550 candidates in total, and published thresholds ranged from 376 to 855 depending on stream and exercise.

---

## On this page

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Date | Round date |
| Stream | Stream 1, 2, 3, or 4 |
| Invitations | Count published for that stream. One cell is `n/a` |
| Score range | Published threshold span, or “No score published” |

Source linked from the page: [Invitations in Arrima for the PSTQ (2026)](https://www.quebec.ca/en/immigration/permanent/skilled-workers/skilled-worker-selection-program/invitation/2026).

The homepage calculator quotes the **24 September 2026** ranges when it compares your estimate with “the last round.”
