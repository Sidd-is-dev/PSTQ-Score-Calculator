# FAQ

**Live:** [https://pstq.getnorthpath.com/faq](https://pstq.getnorthpath.com/faq)

The FAQ page is titled around the questions people arrive with. A shorter set of four answers also sits on the homepage at [/#faq](https://pstq.getnorthpath.com/#faq).

The full page labels each item **Correct** and also prints a **Never say** caution under it. The answers below are the “Correct” text. The cautions are editorial notes on the live page (for example, not to promise a point cutoff). Read them on the site if you are quoting the page. They are not extra scoring rules.

---

## What you do here

1. Separate **points**, **eligibility**, and **invitations** before you read a score
2. Confirm French gates for your stream instead of treating French as a bonus
3. Open [draws](https://pstq.getnorthpath.com/draws) when the question is “what score do I need?”
4. Open [methodology](https://pstq.getnorthpath.com/methodology) when the question is whether the calculator is official

## Answers published on the page

**Is scoring the same as being eligible?** No. Points rank you in the Arrima pool. Eligibility is a separate pass/fail test: stream conditions, French minimums, work experience, status. You can score well and still be ineligible.

**What score do I need to be invited?** There is no fixed pass mark. MIFI invites the top-ranked profiles that match each round’s criteria, so the threshold moves with the round and the stream. Use the last real rounds.

**Do I need French?** For three of the four streams it is a hard gate, not a points bonus: roughly level 5 oral for the intermediate stream and level 7 oral / level 5 written for streams 1 and 3 (TEER 0–2). Confirm the exact levels against the in-force grid. The stream pages state the same gates more tightly. See [STREAMS.md](../STREAMS.md).

**Do I need a job offer?** No. A validated offer is optional in all four streams and worth up to 50 points.

**I was waiting for the PEQ. What now?** The PEQ closed in November 2025. Québec experience and a Québec diploma still score inside the PSTQ. They are not a separate fast track. The expression of interest goes through Arrima.

**Does age count?** Yes. Up to 120 points for the principal applicant, peaking between 20 and 30 and declining to zero at 45. The shape is not the federal CRS age curve. The full year-by-year table is not reprinted in this hub.

**Is a CSQ permanent residence?** No. A CSQ is Québec’s selection certificate. You then apply to IRCC for permanent residence, with its own fees, medicals, security checks, and processing time.

**How does this interact with Express Entry?** Québec runs its own selection outside Express Entry. A CSQ is not a provincial nomination and does not add CRS points.

**How often are draws held?** Roughly every four to six weeks across the four streams. Cadence and the split vary. Nine rounds were held in 2026 as of 24 September. See the draw history.

**Is this calculator official?** No. It is an independent estimate built from the published grid. Only Arrima produces the official score.

**Who checked this?** Point values come from Québec’s published PSTQ grid and the primary sources on the methodology page. Factors marked partial use stated approximations. For eligibility decisions, the page says to consult a licensed immigration professional.

## Homepage quick answers

These four also appear on the calculator page:

| Question | Short answer |
| --- | --- |
| What is the maximum PSTQ score? | 1,400, across Capital humain (520), Réponse aux besoins du marché du travail (700), and Facteurs d'adaptation (180) |
| Is English scored? | No. French only, Échelle québécoise levels 1 to 12, four competencies |
| What are the 4 streams? | Highly qualified (TEER 0–2), intermediate and manual (TEER 3–5), regulated professions, exceptional talent |
| Does a score guarantee an invitation? | No. Draw thresholds vary by stream and are not published in advance |
