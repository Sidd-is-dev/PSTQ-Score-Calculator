# Stream calculator pages

The working calculator is the [homepage](https://pstq.getnorthpath.com/). These four URLs are **stream landings**. Each one states who the stream is for, the TEER band, and the French gate, then links into the full calculator. They do not replace the homepage form.

Shared disclaimer on each landing: the page is an estimate from published PSTQ rules. Confirm NOC, stream, and French level with MIFI. Only Québec issues a selection decision. A score out of 1,400 ranks you in Arrima and does not guarantee an invitation, because each draw sets its own cutoff and that cutoff is not published in advance.

Full gate comparison: [STREAMS.md](../STREAMS.md). Homepage behavior: [HOME.md](HOME.md).

## Exceptional talent

**Live:** [pstq.getnorthpath.com/calculator/exceptional-talent](https://pstq.getnorthpath.com/calculator/exceptional-talent)

| Item | Stated on the page |
| --- | --- |
| Stream | 4 |
| TEER | Any, self-declared |
| Who | Exceptional expertise likely to contribute to Québec’s prosperity |
| Evidence | An achievement on the ministry’s recognized list, or a supporting opinion from MEIE, FRQ, CALQ, or INS |
| Experience | At least 3 years in the last 5 years |
| French gate | **Partial.** “Not stated in the source excerpt we used; verify directly” |

Do not treat a missing French number on this page as “no French requirement.” The site says to verify it.
