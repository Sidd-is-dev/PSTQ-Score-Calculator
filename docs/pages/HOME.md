# Home

**Live:** [https://pstq.getnorthpath.com/](https://pstq.getnorthpath.com/)

The homepage is the **PSTQ calculator**. It estimates a score out of 1,400, assigns a stream, flags hard gates, and compares the estimate with the latest published Arrima round. No account is required.

French: [pstq.getnorthpath.com/fr](https://pstq.getnorthpath.com/fr)

Public preview image: [assets/readme-hero.png](../../assets/readme-hero.png) (the site Open Graph image).

---

## What you do here

1. Read the direct answer: 1,400 points, three sections, French only, four streams, each with a hard minimum
2. Enter age, household, occupation, Québec experience, education, and French levels
3. Read the score, the stream name, the gate message, and the factor breakdown
4. Compare the estimate with the latest round cited on the page
5. Optionally open methodology, draws, a guide, or the consultation form

## On-page sections

| 03 Québec experience and status | General work (max 70 solo / 50 with spouse). Québec work (max 160). Residence / work / study outside Montréal (max 40 / 60 / 20). | Factor breakdown | One row per factor, with `est.` on partial rows, plus highest-impact next steps |
| Disclaimer | Independent estimate: not an official Arrima score, not eligibility, not legal advice |
